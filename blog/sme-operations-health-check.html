The SME Operations Health Check: 10 Questions to Ask Before You Scale

Before you raise money, open a new location or enter a new market, ask these ten questions. The answers show whether your operations are ready to grow.

Riishi KalawatiaNovember 2026 · 5 min read

Small and mid-sized businesses often decide to scale when sales are strong. But growth multiplies whatever is already there: good processes become a competitive advantage, and weak ones become expensive problems. Here are ten questions every business owner should answer before any expansion.

Customers and revenue

1. Do you know your profit per order, product or customer? Not just revenue, but what is left after direct costs, delivery and returns.

2. Can you deliver what you promise, every time? Look at on-time delivery, complaints and refunds for the last three months.

Processes and people

3. Are your key processes written down? If one key person left tomorrow, could someone else run their work next week?

4. Does every critical process have a named owner? Shared responsibility usually means no responsibility.

5. Can your managers run their area without you? If every decision comes back to the founder, growth will stop at the founder's capacity.

Data and finance

6. Do you close your books on time each month? Late numbers mean late decisions.

7. Does your stock in the system match the stock on the shelf? Inventory gaps hide cash and create disappointed customers.

8. Are your records audit-ready? Investors, lenders and regulators will ask, in every country you operate in.

Technology and readiness

9. Does your technology support your process, or fight it? If your team relies on spreadsheets and workarounds, your systems are not ready for twice the volume.

10. What breaks first if volume doubles next month? Every team knows the answer. Fix it before it happens.

How to read your answers

If you answered “no” or “not sure” to three or more questions, your operations need attention before you scale. That is not bad news. It is the cheapest moment to fix them, before growth makes every gap more expensive.

The takeaway

Strong operations do not slow growth down; they make growth safe. Whether your business is in India, the Middle East, the Caribbean or anywhere else, these ten questions are a simple place to start.
