My Method

How I solve operational problems

Different industries, same discipline. I follow every problem from the transaction where it starts to the improvement that fixes it for good.

  1. TransactionStart where the work actually happens: orders, invoices, transfers, dispatches.
  2. ControlCheck that every transaction has a clear rule, an owner and an approval.
  3. ValidationTest the controls with data, not assumptions.
  4. ExceptionFind what does not fit: gaps, variances and red flags.
  5. InsightSeparate facts from opinions and find the root cause.
  6. ImprovementFix the process, measure it, and make the fix stick.

Same method, different industries

Selected engagements. Client names are kept confidential.

Hospitality

Resort food & beverage controls

Challenge: F&B costs with no clear view of where money was leaking.

What I did: Reviewed purchase orders, supplier invoices, store transfers, recipes and portion control, and built red-flag checks for each.

Outcome: A control framework, exception reports, and a roadmap for par levels and booking-based forecasting.

Agriculture & Manufacturing

Tea producer: finance and factory controls

Challenge: Accounts, stock, dispatch, labour and compliance managed without a common control structure.

What I did: Designed a two-level control system with clear escalation for exceptions.

Outcome: Daily checks for the team, and only true exceptions reaching management.

Manufacturing

Pipe manufacturing: material to dispatch

Challenge: Limited visibility of material flow, scrap and dispatch risk.

What I did: Mapped the process end to end and set controls for scrap, traceability, commercial terms and transporters.

Outcome: Reconciled MIS and earlier warning on losses.

E-grocery & Cold Chain

Warehouse SOP audits at scale

Challenge: Keeping standards consistent across fast-moving warehouse and cold chain operations.

What I did: Built SOP audit scorecards, critical-point checks and cash reconciliation, with corrective actions.

Outcome: 2nd rank pan-India for cold chain operations.

Retail

Retail network performance

Challenge: Stores in the same network performing very differently.

What I did: Analysed walk-ins, conversion, average bill value and catchment, backed by field observation and benchmarking.

Outcome: Clear store-level actions and training priorities.

Audit & Loss Prevention

Invoice scrutiny and risk detection

Challenge: Losses hidden in high volumes of invoices and transactions.

What I did: Set up invoice validation and exception detection, with evidence for every finding.

Outcome: Risks found early and corrective actions tracked to closure.

FAQ

Working together

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Startups, SMEs and established companies in retail, food services, cold chain and grocery, e-commerce and fulfillment, manufacturing and business services. Many of my clients are founders preparing to scale.

How does an engagement usually start?

With a short conversation about where your business is today and what is holding it back. From there we agree on the scope, whether that is an operations health check, a focused project or ongoing advisory support.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. Through DBBS Group I work across India and Jamaica, and I support clients internationally. Most work can be done remotely, with on-site visits where they add value.

Can you help with technology and product decisions?

Yes, from the business side. I translate market and operating needs into clear product requirements, work closely with CTOs and technology teams, and run real user-flow testing before launch. I do not write code myself.

What is an operations health check?

A structured review of your customers and revenue, processes and people, data and finance, and technology readiness, based on the ten questions in my free checklist. It gives you a clear list of what to fix before you grow.

What time zones do you work in?

I am based in India (IST, UTC+5:30) and work with teams in the Caribbean (EST, UTC−5). Calls can be scheduled to suit clients in Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.
