FAQ

Working together

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Startups, SMEs and established companies in retail, food services, cold chain and grocery, e-commerce and fulfillment, manufacturing and business services. Many of my clients are founders preparing to scale.

How does an engagement usually start?

With a short conversation about where your business is today and what is holding it back. From there we agree on the scope, whether that is an operations health check, a focused project or ongoing advisory support.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes. Through DBBS Group I work across India and Jamaica, and I support clients internationally. Most work can be done remotely, with on-site visits where they add value.

Can you help with technology and product decisions?

Yes, from the business side. I translate market and operating needs into clear product requirements, work closely with CTOs and technology teams, and run real user-flow testing before launch. I do not write code myself.

What is an operations health check?

A structured review of your customers and revenue, processes and people, data and finance, and technology readiness, based on the ten questions in my free checklist. It gives you a clear list of what to fix before you grow.

What time zones do you work in?

I am based in India (IST, UTC+5:30) and work with teams in the Caribbean (EST, UTC−5). Calls can be scheduled to suit clients in Asia, the Middle East, Europe and the Americas.
