Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026. This policy explains what information this website collects and how it is used.

Who runs this website

This is the personal website of Riishi Kalawatia, based in Bengaluru, India. For any privacy question, email riishi14.kalawatia@gmail.com.

Information you choose to share

When you use the enquiry form, the website assistant, email or WhatsApp, you may share your name, organisation, email address, country, phone number, your message and your answers to the assistant’s questions. These details are not stored on this website; they are sent by email to Riishi Kalawatia through Formspree, a form-delivery service.

Website assistant. The “Ask Riishi’s assistant” chat is automated: it asks a set of questions and does not use artificial intelligence to generate replies. Your answers, and the summary it prepares, are sent to Riishi only after you tick the consent box, so he can prepare for your call.

How your information is used

Your details are used only to reply to your enquiry and to discuss the work you asked about. They are not sold, rented or shared with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

Enquiry-form messages and assistant summaries are delivered by Formspree, which processes them to send the email. This website also loads fonts from Google Fonts and links to LinkedIn, WhatsApp and company websites. These services have their own privacy policies. If website analytics are added in future, this policy will be updated to describe them.

How long information is kept

Enquiry emails and messages are kept only as long as needed to respond and to maintain a record of business conversations.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete the personal information you have sent at any time by emailing riishi14.kalawatia@gmail.com. Visitors from regions with specific data protection laws, such as the European Union, the United Kingdom or India, can use this address to exercise their rights under those laws.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
